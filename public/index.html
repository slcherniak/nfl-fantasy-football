Standings, Adjusted for Reality

Record is what the schedule gave you. All-Play is your record if you'd faced all 11 opponents every week — the schedule-proof version. Luck is actual wins minus the wins all-play says you earned. Odds come from 10,000 simulated season endings.

The Luck Quadrant

Expected wins (from all-play scoring) against actual wins. On the diagonal, the standings tell the truth. Above it, the schedule has been kind; below it, you've been robbed.

Rosters vs Managers

Best possible points per week (roster strength) against the share of it the lineup actually captured. Right side drafted well; top edge sets the lineup well.

Floors and Ceilings

Worst week against best week. A high floor steals wins on off weeks; a high ceiling wins playoff games. Low floor with a high ceiling is the true boom-or-bust profile.

Scoring Distributions

Every week each team has played, one dot per game. The bar spans their floor to their ceiling; the notch is their median. The dashed line is the league median.

The Playoff Odds Race

Playoff probability recomputed after every completed week, each time simulating the rest of the season 3,000 times from that point's records and scoring profiles.

This Week's Leverage

What the next matchup is worth: playoff odds if you win vs if you lose, simulated both ways. The longer the bar, the bigger the game.

Seed Odds

Not just "in or out" — which seed. In a no-reseed bracket the 3-seed and 6-seed live different lives.

Week-by-Week Trajectories

Each team against the league median (dashed). Same scale everywhere, so shapes compare honestly.

Power Rank History

The composite power ranking recomputed after every week. Rank 1 at the top — watch the collapses.

Bench Crimes

The single worst legal start/sit call each week — the one bench player who should have been in, by name. Totals are cumulative points lost to the worst weekly call.

The Transaction Desk

Every move graded by what the player actually scored in the lineup after it — points rotting on the bench don't count. Re-graded automatically each week.
