The Film Room

Standings, Adjusted for Reality

Record is what the schedule gave you. Luck is actual wins minus the wins your scores earned against the whole league (all-play). Odds come from 10,000 simulated season endings.

The Luck Quadrant

Expected wins (from all-play scoring) against actual wins. On the diagonal, the standings tell the truth. Above it, the schedule has been kind; below it, you've been robbed.

Rosters vs Managers

Best possible points per week (roster strength) against the share of it the lineup actually captured. Right side drafted well; top edge sets the lineup well.

Boom or Bust

Scoring average against week-to-week volatility. High scorers who swing hard are live playoff underdogs; steady ones are safe but capped.

Scoring Distributions

Every week each team has played, one dot per game. The bar spans their floor to their ceiling; the notch is their median. The dashed line is the league median.

The Playoff Odds Race

Playoff probability recomputed after every completed week, each time simulating the rest of the season 3,000 times from that point's records and scoring profiles.

Week-by-Week Trajectories

Each team against the league median (dashed). Same scale everywhere, so shapes compare honestly.

All-Play Domination Grid

Row team's weekly-score record against each column team across every week, even when they never met. Darker means more dominant. Teams ordered by all-play wins.
